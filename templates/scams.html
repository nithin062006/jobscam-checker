Common fake job scams in India

Know the patterns, and you can spot most fake offers in under a minute.

1. The registration fee scam

You get a great offer, then are asked to pay a "registration", "security deposit", "training kit", or "laptop" fee. After you pay, they ask for more or disappear.

Rule: real employers don't charge you to hire you.

2. The task / prepaid-task scam

You're told to like videos, rate places, or complete small tasks to earn money. The first small payouts are real, then you must "deposit" money to unlock bigger earnings, and the money is never returned.

3. The fake offer letter

A convincing offer letter from a real company arrives after a short chat on WhatsApp or Telegram, with no interview. Check that the email domain matches the company's website, and confirm the offer through the company's official careers page.

4. The data-theft scam

"HR" asks for your Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, OTP, or UPI PIN to "verify" or "process" your joining. Never share OTPs or PINs. Share ID documents only with a verified employer, and only for a real offer.

5. The too-good-to-be-true work-from-home offer

High pay for typing, data entry, or part-time work with no experience required. Compare the pay with what that role actually earns.

Quick checklist before you reply

  • Is the email from the company's own domain (not Gmail or Yahoo)?
  • Does the company exist on LinkedIn, the MCA registry, and the official website?
  • Did you actually apply, and was there a real interview?
  • Is anyone asking for money, OTPs, or PINs? If yes, stop.

If you've been scammed

  • Call 1930 (national cyber crime helpline) immediately if money was lost.
  • Report at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Tell your bank at once, and save all chats, receipts, and numbers.
